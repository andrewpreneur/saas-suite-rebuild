## 0.6 Gap analysis — pages that should exist and don't

Ranked by likely value.

1. **`/highlevel-saas-mode-guide`** — a definitive pillar guide to SaaS mode (setup, pricing, rebilling, margins, churn). Enormous informational demand, no page targets it, and it's the natural internal-linking hub for everything else. **Highest value.**
2. **`/gohighlevel-snapshots`** or a snapshots explainer — "snapshot" is one of the highest-intent ecosystem terms and the word appears in your comparison copy but has no page.
3. **`/a2p-registration`** — A2P 10DLC is a genuine pain point, you already sell it as a service under Agency Support, and it has strong search demand with weak competition.
4. **More `/alternatives/*` pages** — Growthable, SaaS Prodigy, HL Pro Tools support-desk specifically, Extendly Quickstart, "build it yourself vs buy". `[INFERRED]` — cheap to produce, highest conversion rate on the site.
5. **More `/for/*` niches** — dentists, home services (HVAC/plumbing/roofing), gyms/fitness, insurance agents, restaurants. The template already exists; each is a new keyword surface.
6. **`/gohighlevel-white-label-cost`** — a dedicated cost/calculator page. "How much does it cost" queries convert and currently land on generic `/pricing`.
7. **`/vs/build-vs-buy`** — "should I build my own onboarding/support for my HighLevel SaaS" — captures the pre-solution searcher.
8. **Case studies / results pages** — you have zero. This is the biggest trust and E-E-A-T gap on the site. `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]` — do you have named agencies willing to be featured?
9. **`/glossary`** — SaaS-mode terminology glossary. Cheap, and glossary entries get cited disproportionately by AI answer engines.
10. **Pages with no clear keyword target today:** `/hierarchy`, `/brand-kit`, `/bonuses`, `/offers`, `/switch`, `/gift`, `/summit`, `/special`, `/promo`. My recommendation is `noindex` on all of them rather than trying to rank them.
